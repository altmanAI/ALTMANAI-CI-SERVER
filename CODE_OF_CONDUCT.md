# Code of Conduct

AltmanAI expects professional, respectful, evidence-based collaboration.

Contributors must:

- engage ideas without attacking people
- protect private and security-sensitive information
- disclose conflicts of interest
- distinguish verified behavior from plans or claims
- accept human review and accountable decision-making

Harassment, threats, impersonation, credential exposure, deliberate misinformation, and attempts to bypass governance controls are not acceptable.
